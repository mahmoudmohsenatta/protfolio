## Description
One credit membership. Endless Saudi experiences.

Discover, book, and check in to Riyadh and Jeddah's top fitness studios, yoga houses, padel clubs, music academies, pottery labs, and family venues — all from a single credit wallet.

- **Flexible Credit Plans:** Subscribe to 30, 60, or 100 credits/month with rollover perks and quick top-ups.
- **Map & Curated Filters:** Filter by Riyadh/Jeddah districts, time of day, women-only sessions, and family experiences.
- **Instant QR Check-In:** Show your booking QR code at reception — no cards or paperwork.
- **Nabd AI Concierge:** Ask in English or Arabic (e.g. *"Calm activity Friday evening in Jeddah"*) for instant recommendations.
- **Local Payments:** Supports mada, Apple Pay, STC Pay, and credit cards with clear 15% VAT invoicing.
